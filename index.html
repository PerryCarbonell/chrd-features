Signal

Your real-time discovery feed organized by proximity and time.

Signal shows three types of content—all grouped by how far away they are happening right now:

  • → Listening— People nearby who matched your taste. See what they're listening to in real time. 🎧
  • → Scene— Live events, venues, performances, and things happening around you. Join a coffee chat, catch a vinyl shop, or discover a park jam. 📍
  • → Connect— Host or join a music conversation party. Start one about any topic (song, artist, video, event) and match with others in your vibe. 🎵

Everything updates by proximity: soonest first (2–5 mins), then farther out (5–15 mins), then beyond. Swipe through to see what's buzzing near you, follow the audio clips, tap to join.
