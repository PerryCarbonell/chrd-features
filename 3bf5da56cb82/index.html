CHRD is real-time social discovery for music lovers.

Find your people, connect, and find your scene.

CHRD looks at what you love (songs, artists, albums, gigs) and helps you bump into people nearby who love the same things.

Save a song you care about; when someone close by is into it too, you might get a small, unexpected moment between you—a shared mood, not a dating pitch.

Your feed is split into three rings of distance: who's closest, who's elsewhere in town today, and who's farther out—so what you see lines up with how near or far people are, not just what they like. All prioritised based on your musical taste.
