CHRD

Discover music together — nearby, now.

CHRD is real-time social discovery for music lovers.

Find your people, connect, and find your scene.

The walkthroughs below cover Home, Signal, Search, Profile, and Artists.

Home

Feed

Your main timeline — swipe through posts, clips, and moments from your crew.

  • Full-screen vertical feed with like, comment, and share
  • Collapsed hashtag — tap to reveal caption and author
  • Video, photo, and text posts; artists can share audio posts
  • Long press to view the post without distraction, tap to return

Landscape

Rotate your phone to watch a post in cinema mode — focused video with actions on the side.

  • Landscape clips fill the screen; portrait posts use a split layout
  • Hashtag, caption, and engagement stay within reach
  • On grids, turn back to portrait to keep browsing

Signal

Landing

Signal opens on your real-time bento feed — three time bands, tiles sorted by travel time.

  • Right here · Around town · Coming up — what's nearest first
  • Closest tiles top-left; later ones toward bottom-right — ordered by time
  • Find your people, share live moments and hang out with others, connect in Rooms

Actions

Tap + for four ways to put yourself on the map:

  • Update your signal — it helps find your people
  • Subculture — it helps find your people once again
  • Something's on — solo live now or join others
  • Rooms — host a virtual party and connect with others

Update your signal

Keep your taste row fresh — the more current, the more meaningful the connections.

  • Fave songs, artists, albums, and shows in one canvas
  • Strength meter on the row shows how complete your signal is

Find your people

One match tile per time tab — shared songs, artists, and gigs rotate inside the box.

  • Avatars, venue headline, travel time, and signal-strength rings on each slide
  • Tap the tile → sheet with Songs · Artists · Albums · Shows
  • Send ♪ on overlaps you share; rose replies when someone nods back

Solo & meetups

Real-world moments in the feed — broadcast solo or join a hang someone posted.

  • Solo live — tap a #LIVE tile; 15 min LIVE NOW, then Ongoing
  • Meetup — plan or join a hang (#HANGOUT); up to 1 h on Signal
  • Photo/video in the composer; tiles sit beside matches and Rooms by travel time

Rooms

Virtual listening parties in the bento feed.

  • Party name → On: topic → live headcount (e.g. Jazz Lovers)
  • LIVE NOW or Starts in X min; lobby opens ≤5 min early
  • Tap Join; host starts the room when it's time

Search

Discovery & subculture

Search is a discovery grid — browse posts, people, and scenes without leaving the tab.

  • Bento tiles for posts, Signal moments, and culture picks
  • Subculture filter narrows the grid to your scene (e.g. #INDIE)
  • Tap a tile to open in place; back returns to the grid

Profile

Poster & Journal

Your profile opens on a full-screen print-style Poster — type and image fill the frame.

  • Poster: handle, avatar, bio, On Rotation (fans) or catalog (artists)
  • About → Journal: dated activity and scene memories alongside Bio
  • About → Music to edit taste; optional looping background video

Next Gigs

List the shows you're hitting — dated entries on your profile Music tab.

  • Add upcoming gigs with venue and date
  • Mark yourself going and see who's in your crew
  • Share what's on your calendar before the night

Themes

Make your profile yours — colour, wallpaper, type, and optional header video.

  • Pick a theme colour and wallpaper pattern
  • Choose bio typography and avatar style
  • Add a looping background video to your header

Artists

Artists

Browse artists from the capsule nav — each opens a full artist Poster.

  • Directory scroll of live and upcoming artists on CHRD
  • Tap through to artist Bio, catalog, and posts
  • Claimed artists show Gigs, Popular, and Albums on their Poster
