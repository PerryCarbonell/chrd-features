CHRD

Discover music together — nearby, now.

CHRD is real-time social discovery for music lovers.

Find your people, connect, and find your scene.

The walkthroughs below cover Home, Signal, and Profile.

Home

Feed

Your main timeline — swipe through posts, clips, and moments from your crew.

  • Full-screen vertical feed with like, comment, and share
  • Tap through video, audio, and text posts in one place
  • Where you land when you open CHRD

Landscape

Rotate your phone to watch a post in cinema mode — focused video with actions on the side.

  • Landscape clips fill the screen; portrait posts use a split layout
  • Hashtag, caption, and engagement stay within reach
  • On grids, turn back to portrait to keep browsing

Signal

Overview

Your real-time discovery feed — organized by proximity and time.

  • Listening — taste matches nearby, live
  • Scene — events and moments around you
  • Connect — drop into a music conversation

Soonest first (2–5 mins), then farther out (5–15 mins), then beyond. Swipe, follow clips, tap to join.

Create

What's on — post Listening, Scene, or Connect from Signal.

  • Share a track you're into
  • Drop a local scene or hang
  • Start a Connect party on a topic

Listen

See who's nearby matching your taste — and what they're playing.

  • Real-time listening cards
  • Taste intensity at a glance
  • Tap in when the vibe fits

Scene

Live moments around you — venues, hangs, and things kicking off.

  • Sorted by how close and how soon
  • Open a scene for the full detail
  • Join or follow what's on

Scene + audio

Same scene detail with sound — hear the clip while you read the room.

  • Audio plays with the preview
  • Keep context: where, when, who's there

Connect

Drop into a live music conversation — open parties in your Nearby feed.

  • Join on a shared taste or topic (e.g. Jazz Lovers)
  • 15-minute room with rotating topics and reactions
  • Ephemeral chat — tap Join from the feed when the vibe fits

Profile

On Rotation

Show what you're into — the artists and tracks on repeat right now.

  • Update your top artists from the Music tab
  • Visitors see your taste at a glance on your profile
  • A living snapshot of what's spinning this week

Next Gigs

List the shows you're hitting — dated entries on your profile Music tab.

  • Add upcoming gigs with venue and date
  • Mark yourself going and see who's in your crew
  • Share what's on your calendar before the night

Themes

Make your profile yours — colour, wallpaper, type, and optional header video.

  • Pick a theme colour and wallpaper pattern
  • Choose bio typography and avatar style
  • Add a looping background video to your header
